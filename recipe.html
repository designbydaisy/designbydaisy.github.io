Lara Jean's "Love Letter" Cherry Turnovers

"Cherry Turnover DSC05996" by kurmanstaff is licensed under CC BY 2.0.

"I'd rather make my boo cake than get a bouquet." - Lara Jean

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet frozen puff pastry (thawed)
  • 1 cup cherry pie filling
  • 1 egg (lightly beaten for egg wash)
  • 1/4 teaspoon almond extract
  • Coarse sugar for sprinkling

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F and line a pan with parchment paper.
  2. Cut puff pastry into four equal squares.
  3. Mix the cherry filling with almond extract.
  4. Place a spoonful of filling on each square, fold into a triangle, and seal edges with a fork.
  5. Brush with egg wash and sprinkle with sugar.
  6. Bake for 18 minutes or until puffed and golden brown.

Background: Inspired by the To All the Boys I've Loved Before series, these turnovers are a classic Covey sister favorite.
